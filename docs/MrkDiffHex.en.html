FAQ

A few things to know

Which Macs are supported?

MrkDiff Hex requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. It supports both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Are my files uploaded?

Local files are compared on your Mac. You do not need to upload them for comparison. Purchasing and license activation use a network connection.

Can I merge text changes?

You can apply a selected difference to either side. This is not a three-way merge tool. Merging is unavailable for reformatted comparison views, such as JSON and YAML.

How is this different from the free MrDiff?

MrDiff is a free command-line tool for checking differences in a terminal. MrkDiff Hex is a paid GUI app for inspecting binary and text files side by side.
